Methodology & Disclaimers

Research only. This terminal presents stored data and model output for study. It never places orders, and nothing on it is investment advice.

Model event score is an uncalibrated machine estimate of a +10% high within 20 sessions. It is a rough lean, not a forecast and not the odds of a trade working out. The system's own graded record is shown alongside it and is usually far lower.

Price data is daily end-of-day only. Bars describe recent behaviour; they do not predict the next move. Every panel states its source bar date where it matters.

Position sizing is a fixed-risk research estimate limited by planned stop risk and maximum allocation. Gaps, slippage, fees, and taxes can increase losses beyond the planned amount.

Historical results come from forward validation with a visible sample size. Small samples are labelled as such and should not be read as an edge.

Press the ? beside any label, or click a heading, for the plain words behind that specific number.
